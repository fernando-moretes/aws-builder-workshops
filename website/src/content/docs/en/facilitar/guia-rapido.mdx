## One week before

- [ ] Topic, format, capacity, duration and accessibility confirmed with the organizers.
- [ ] Prerequisites sent, saying what needs an AWS account and how to follow along without one.
- [ ] Audio, terminal zoom and connection tested.
- [ ] Offline copy of the material.
- [ ] Timed rehearsal. An estimate is not a measured duration.

## On the day

<Steps>

1. **Open with three questions.** What decision do you need to make at work? What have you already tried? What would make this session worth your time?

2. **Form pairs,** one who builds and one who reviews, swapping roles.

3. **An unanswered question goes on a list,** with an owner and a source. No invented answers.

4. **Close with the deliverable:** one decision, the evidence, one risk and one next test.

</Steps>

## If the demo fails

Say it failed and use the rehearsal results, labeled as such. **Never present recorded output as a live call.** The whole Session 2 activity works on paper, and the console works offline once loaded.

## With your agent's help

With the MCP connected, use the `facilitate_workshop` prompt, or ask:

> Prepare the facilitation of the arquitetura-sob-pressao workshop: a minute-by-minute agenda, three questions for the group, what to check beforehand and the fallback plan.

## Afterwards

- Answer the pending questions, with sources.
- Publish only real results, with permission when people are involved.
- Count participants and completed exercises separately.
