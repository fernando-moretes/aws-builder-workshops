An open kit for people just getting started with AWS. Instead of watching a class, you test something small, check what the AI said and leave with something to show.

![The four steps: real question, small experiment, evidence and article](/aws-builder-workshops/img/routine.en.svg)

## What is here

| Piece | What it is for | What you need |
|---|---|---|
| [Three missions](/aws-builder-workshops/en/comece/escolha/) | Learn AWS by testing, about 1 hour each | A browser and an AI. An AWS account is optional at first |
| [From test to article](/aws-builder-workshops/en/trilhas/primeiro-experimento/) | Turn what you saw into an article of your own | Ready-made templates |
| [Real examples](/aws-builder-workshops/en/trilhas/exemplos/) | See the prompts that were run and checked | Just read |
| [Your AI in the IDE](/aws-builder-workshops/en/agentes/kiro/) | Have the AI guide you in Kiro and other IDEs | Node.js or Docker |
| [Two sessions](/aws-builder-workshops/en/facilitar/dois-encontros/) | Run it with a group | Ready-made script |
| [Advanced](/aws-builder-workshops/en/trilhas/lab-triagem/) | Evaluate agents, queue capacity, Bedrock | For later |

## What to expect, and what not to expect

:::note[It is]
Independent study material, with steps that were really tested and limits written down.
:::

:::caution[It is not]
Official AWS training, a guarantee of certification or of getting into programs, or a production-ready architecture. See the [known limits](/aws-builder-workshops/en/referencia/limites/).
:::
