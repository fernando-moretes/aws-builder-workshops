## What to look for

<Steps>

1. **Find the settlement line.** The lookup failed, and `metrics` is `null`, not zero.

2. **Read the component's state.** It's `unknown`. An unavailable lookup doesn't mean the component is down, and it doesn't mean it's healthy either.

3. **Look at the three counts.** `model_calls`, `aws_calls` and `mutations` are all 0. This is a teaching simulation, not a model's answer.

4. **Now think:** if an agent answered "settlement is normal", what evidence would you ask for?

</Steps>

:::tip[In real life]
When we ran these cases with a real model, it recognized the failure in A2, but classified other metrics as normal with no SLO to back that up. [See the evaluation](/aws-builder-workshops/en/trilhas/bedrock-opcional/).
:::
