## The rules of the game

<div class="bw-grid">
  <div class="bw-card"><strong>You test</strong><span>Every mission starts from a real question and has an expected and an observed result.</span></div>
  <div class="bw-card"><strong>The AI is a tutor</strong><span>It explains, asks and reviews. The text is yours, and you can explain every line.</span></div>
  <div class="bw-card"><strong>Always check</strong><span>Even when the AI is right. Opening the source is what turns an answer into evidence.</span></div>
  <div class="bw-card"><strong>No surprises</strong><span>Before each step in the cloud, you see the cost and how to delete everything.</span></div>
