# Facilitation

Three independent 120-minute workshops, for pairs with alternating builder and reviewer roles. Use `list_workshops`, and `get_doc` with `facilitador`, `atividades` or `modelos`.

## Before

- Confirm topic, format, capacity, duration and accessibility with the organizer.
- Say what needs an AWS account and offer a way to follow without one.
- Time a rehearsal. Do not record an estimate as a measured duration.
- Keep an offline copy of everything.

## During

- Open with: what decision do you need to make at work? What have you tried? What would make this worth your time?
- Questions that need research go on a return list, with an owner and a source. No invented answers.
- Never present recorded output as a live call. If the demo fails, say so and use the rehearsal results, labeled as such.

## After

- Success: each person presents a decision, its evidence, one risk and one next test.
- Publish only real results, with permission when people are involved. Count participants and completed exercises separately. Likes are not a result.
