# First experiment: real question, small experiment, evidence, article

For beginners. Needs no cloud account. Takes two sessions: one to test, one to write.

## Session 1: from question to evidence

1. **Start from a real question.** Ask what confused them recently. Reject topics that are only a service name until there is a question inside.
2. **Create the folder:** `npx -y moretes-builder-workshops experiment my-experiment --lang pt` (or the `start_experiment` tool). It creates the proposal, evidence log, article skeleton and checklist.
3. **Fill in the proposal.** The person writes it. Push back on goals that can't be proven in two hours, and ask for the hypothesis before any test.
4. **Run the smallest test.** Prefer local and fictional data. Good starting points: `queue_capacity`, or changing one input and predicting the result first.
5. **Log as it happens.** Expected and observed, including failures.

## Session 2: from evidence to article

1. The person writes the first draft from the evidence log, in their own words.
2. You review, in this order: can someone repeat it? Does every number come from the test or a source? Does it say what it doesn't prove? Is there a conditional recommendation?
3. Go through `CHECKLIST.md` together. Ask them to open each link.
4. They publish wherever they want and share the link.

## How to review without taking over

- Ask "how did you observe this?" instead of correcting the claim.
- Point to the sentence and name the problem. Let them rewrite it.
- Suggesting a reworded sentence is fine only if they ask, and only one at a time.
- If the text sounds generated, say so: ask what they would say to a colleague.

## Common gaps

| Sign | Question to ask |
|---|---|
| "X is faster" | Faster than what, measured how? |
| "It's always cheaper" | At what volume? Where is the price from, and from when? |
| No failures mentioned | What went wrong the first time? |
| Service list with no decision | What would make you choose differently? |
| Screenshots with account data | What needs to be removed from this image? |
