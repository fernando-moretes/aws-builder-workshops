# Experiment proposal

Fill this in before opening any tool. A rough draft is fine: the point is to have a direction.

- **Real question:** what do you want to find out, in your own words?
- **Audience:** who would this help? Be specific: "someone in their first job who has never used queues" beats "developers".
- **Problem:** what can't this person do or decide today?
- **Goal:** what can you prove in two hours or less?
- **Hypothesis:** what do you expect to happen, before testing?
- **How I'll test it:** the smallest possible exercise. Prefer running locally with fictional data.
- **What counts as evidence:** which number, output or behavior confirms or refutes the hypothesis?
- **Expected cost:** zero is a great answer. If you use the cloud, what is the maximum, and what gets deleted at the end?
